# Brag Plan: Shion

## What is this app?
Shion is the site of a Japanese kaiseki & omakase restaurant in Kebayoran Baru, South Jakarta: three dinner courses (Ume, Take, Matsu), a hinoki counter, online booking with a QRIS payment step, and private weddings where every guest gets a digital invitation with an entry QR.

## The angle
The name is the ad. "Shion" is the purple aster, and in the Japanese language of flowers it means *I will not forget you*. The site's own line: "Kami memasak supaya malam Anda di sini juga begitu." The video compresses one evening at Shion, 18.00 to 22.00, into twenty seconds, and ends on the promise in the name. Quiet, premium, food-first. No hype words, no "best sushi in Jakarta".

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Black-aubergine screen. The kanji 紫苑 brushes in large in lilac, then one line settles under it: "Artinya: aku tidak akan melupakanmu." A viewer scrolling past sees a single beautiful glyph and a sentence that reads like a love note, not an ad.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The chef's hands placing uni and tuna nigiri (hero photo), with SHION set in the site's heavy wide uppercase and "Kaiseki & omakase · Jakarta Selatan".
- One evening, hour by hour: 18.00 Sakizuke, 19.00 Owan, 20.00 Yakimono, 21.00 Shokuji, 22.00 Kanmi, each with its own photo from the site, a clock line advancing.
- Three journeys: Ume 梅, Take 竹, Matsu 松 cards arrive one by one with their real prices; a tap on "Pesan Matsu" lands a booking confirmation.
- Weddings: a phone shows a guest invitation (fictional couple "Rina & Dimas" from the demo data) with the entry QR.

## Outro / punchline
"Malamnya selesai, kenangannya belum." (the site's own closing line for the Kanmi course), then 紫苑 SHION, "Pesan meja Anda", "Kebayoran Baru, Jakarta Selatan · Selasa–Minggu".

## User flow worth showing
Pick a course (Matsu card) → tap "Pesan Matsu" → confirmation "Reservasi SHN-4821 terkonfirmasi". Secondary flow: wedding guest opens invitation → sees entry QR.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet premium restaurant film, one evening in twenty seconds
- Interpretation: few scenes with long holds, slow crossfades and soft scale drifts on photos, mixed case serif copy, restraint as the luxury signal; audio is a low steady bed with 2-3 soft accents.

## Format: vertical — 1080x1920
Chosen for Instagram Reels / TikTok / Stories ads.

## Duration: 23s

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #2a1a2b (aubergine, --color-2)
- Paper: #f0e2d8 (cream, --color-1)
- Accent: #b78cf2 (lilac, --color-3); deep accent #6e3b9e (--color-4)
- Text: #f0e2d8 on dark, #2a1a2b on cream
- Display font: TikTok Sans 900, width 125%, uppercase (site's hero/invitation names)
- Serif: Brygada 1918 (headings, prices, times, uppercase)
- Kanji: Yuji Syuku (紫苑, 梅 竹 松, 結)
- Strongest visual element: real food photography (hero.jpg, h1–h5.jpg) framed hard-edged, no rounded corners, on aubergine and cream

## Copy rules
- Indonesian only, taken from the site's own copy.
- Do not show the placeholder address "Jl. Contoh Raya No. 88", the placeholder phone, or any email. The restaurant is fictional (portfolio project); show only "Kebayoran Baru, Jakarta Selatan".
- Photos are Flickr CC BY 2.0: end card carries a small credit line "Foto: Flickr, CC BY 2.0 · kredit lengkap di keterangan"; full names go in share copy.

## Share copy (draft)

## Audio direction
- Role: warm, steady bed under quiet imagery
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12 (steady and clean; best fit for polished among bundled tracks)
- Music treatment: start at 0, volume ~0.28 (lower than default to stay calm), 0.6s fade-in, 1.2s fade-out at the end
- Music cue guidance: preset read (109.96 BPM). Strong cues in window: 8.74, 13.11, 17.47, 18.56, 22.37, 22.93. Lock "Tiga perjalanan rasa" title near 13.11 and the final 紫苑 SHION lockup near 22.37 (±0.15s). Hour sequence 7.6–12.8s may sit on every other beat (~1.09s spacing) so each hour label holds ≥0.8s.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; the lilac glow behind the kanji and the photo vignette may breathe with RMS. No visualizer graphics.
- SFX posture: sparse (polished). Soft wood knock for the hook kanji, soft card place for course cards (first and last only), one click on the "Pesan Matsu" tap, one soft bell on the final lockup.
- Restraint rule: no whooshes, no glitch, nothing louder than the bed by much; never score every hour tick.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Bunga — 3.6s
Aubergine background. 紫苑 (Yuji Syuku, lilac, very large) brushes/fades in with a slight upward drift and soft lilac glow. Small caps label above: "SHION · BUNGA ASTER UNGU". Then the line settles under the kanji: "Artinya: aku tidak akan melupakanmu." (holds ≥1.6s fully visible).
Sequential/interaction: label → kanji → line, in that order.
Audio intent: hush, then warmth arrives.
Audio-coupled idea: soft wood knock as the kanji lands.
Music: fade in from silence.
Transition mood: soft → Scene 2 (kanji dissolves into the photo)

### Scene 2 — Counter — 4.0s
Full-bleed hero.jpg (chef's hands placing uni & tuna nigiri) with slow push-in. Bottom third darkens. "SHION" in TikTok Sans 900 wide uppercase, cream, large; under it "Kaiseki & omakase · Jakarta Selatan" (Brygada uppercase small). Holds ≥2s settled.
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: confidence; let the bed carry.
Audio-coupled idea: none
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Satu malam — 5.4s
Title at top on aubergine: "Satu malam omakase" with small "18.00 → 22.00". Below, a tall photo frame cycles h1→h5 with crossfades while a vertical time rail advances: 18.00 Sakizuke, 19.00 Owan, 20.00 Yakimono, 21.00 Shokuji, 22.00 Kanmi. Current hour bright cream, past hours dim. Each hour label ≥0.9s.
Sequential/interaction: yes — five hours advance one by one, photo swaps with each.
Audio intent: gentle forward motion.
Audio-coupled idea: hours on every other beat; no SFX per tick.
Transition mood: soft → Scene 4 (cut to cream paper)

### Scene 4 — Tiga perjalanan rasa — 4.6s
Cream background (site paper). Title "Tiga perjalanan rasa" (Brygada uppercase, aubergine) lands near the 13.11s cue. Three rows arrive one by one like the site's course table, separated by hairlines: "UME 梅 · 6 course · Rp 750.000", "TAKE 竹 · 8 course · Rp 1.150.000", "MATSU 松 · 10 course · Rp 1.850.000". Each row has the outlined "Pesan … →" button from the site. A finger/cursor taps "Pesan Matsu": button fills dark from the bottom (the site's hover fill), then a confirmation bar slides up: "Reservasi SHN-4821 terkonfirmasi" with a small check. Rows hold all together ≥1.4s before the tap.
Sequential/interaction: yes — rows one by one, then simulated tap, then confirmation.
Audio intent: tactile, precise.
Audio-coupled idea: soft card place on first and last row; one click on the tap.
Transition mood: clean slide → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Wedding — 3.0s
Aubergine. Left/top copy: "Seluruh restoran, untuk satu cerita." A phone frame rises showing the guest invitation: "RINA & DIMAS" in the invitation's heavy uppercase, lilac "&", 結, date line, and an entry QR card "QR masuk · 2 orang".
Sequential/interaction: phone rises, QR draws in.
Audio intent: tender, one small sparkle at most.
Audio-coupled idea: optional light glass clink when the QR appears (very low).
Transition mood: soft → Scene 6

### Scene 6 — Kenangan — 2.4s + Lockup
Aubergine. "Malamnya selesai," then "kenangannya belum." (two lines, Brygada, cream; second line lilac). Hold 1.4s. Then lockup lands near 22.37s: 紫苑 (Yuji Syuku, lilac) + "SHION" (TikTok Sans wide) + "Pesan meja Anda" + "Kebayoran Baru, Jakarta Selatan · Selasa–Minggu". Tiny credit line at the bottom: "Foto: Flickr, CC BY 2.0 · kredit lengkap di keterangan". Hold to the end.
Sequential/interaction: two lines, then lockup.
Audio intent: resolve; soft bell on the lockup, music fades.
Audio-coupled idea: bell at lockup.
Transition mood: end

Scene timing: 3.6 + 4.0 + 5.4 + 4.6 + 3.0 + 2.4 (+ lockup hold to 23.0) ≈ 23.0s

**Music mood for this video:** steady, warm, understated
**Audio summary:** a low clean bed fades up under the kanji, moves the evening forward without calling attention to itself, and resolves on one soft bell under the final lockup.
